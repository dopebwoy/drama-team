About us

We are the Drama Team family, Guardian of the heaven , Blessed parteners.

Welcome to Drama Team Family! We are a family of young people who love music, dancing, and praising God through gospel songs. We come together to share our talents, help each other, and enjoy art and performances.
A Short Story About Drama Team Family: Drama Team Family started in 2018 with a small group of seven girls. As time went on, the family grew bigger. Many members have now finished school, but Drama Team Family is still in their hearts. The love, discipline, confidence, public speaking skills, and other good values they learned are still part of their daily lives. Every year, new students from different schools can join Drama Team Family if they wish. Our doors are always open to welcome new members! To keep the memories of our past performances, parties, and other events at ES Rugabano, we have created an official website called **DRAMA TEAM FAMILY**. Through this website, members of different generations can share, upload, and download photos taken during these special events. As time goes on, we hope to make the website even better. In the future, we may also add our Saturday live meetings to the website so that members can join and watch them online from anywhere.

Our mission

We believe that through gospel music and dance, we can inspire people, spread joy, and bring hope to others. This platform was created to help our family share memories, pictures, and important moments from our journey together through images shared.

What we stand for

  • Family: We support and care for one another.
  • Talent: We believe every person has something beautiful to share.
  • Unity: We grow stronger when we work together.
  • Worship: We use music and dance to glorify God and inspire others.
