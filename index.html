Welcome

Welcome to Drama Team Family, a home for our gallery, creative fellowship, and shared memories.

Drama Team Family is based at École Secondaire Rugabano, a public government-aided secondary school in Rugabano Sector, Karongi District, Western Province, Rwanda. Students who join the family are called Umufamiliye. The family is organized into cabin groups led by cabin leaders called Umukomite, under Père and Mère as grand cabin leaders.

Guardians of Heaven, we are blessed partners. 💚
