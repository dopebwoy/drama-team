About us

We are the Drama Team family, guardians of heaven and blessed partners.

Welcome to Drama Team Family! We are a family of young people who love music, dancing, and praising God through gospel songs. We come together to share our talents, support one another, and enjoy art and performances.

A short story about our family: Drama Team Family started in 2018 with a small group of seven girls. The family grew over time. Many members have finished school, but the love, discipline, confidence, public speaking skills, and values they learned remain part of their lives.

Each year, new students from different schools can join. This website keeps memories of performances, parties, and other events at ES Rugabano, so members across generations can share, upload, and download photos.

The website now also has a LiveKit video meeting room. Members can open Live meeting and join from anywhere using a camera and microphone. Umukomite can manage the room and remove Umufamiliye participants when needed.

Our mission

We believe that through gospel music and dance, we can inspire people, spread joy, and bring hope to others. This platform was created to help our family share memories, pictures, and important moments from our journey together through the images we share.

What we stand for

  • Family: We support and care for one another.
  • Talent: We believe every person has something beautiful to share.
  • Unity: We grow stronger when we work together.
  • Worship: We use music and dance to glorify God and inspire others.

A few simple steps

How to Use the Platform (Member Guide)

Choose what you want to do below. Each page is designed to make it easy for family members to take part and stay in touch.

1. Shared Memories & Gallery (store.html)

  1. Open Gallery. If asked to sign in, use your family name and member password.
  2. Choose Photos or Video, then browse the shared memories by title or date.
  3. To add a rehearsal or gathering memory, choose Upload, select your photo or video, and add a clear title before uploading.

2. Live Family Meetings (meeting.html)

  1. Open Live meeting on your phone or computer.
  2. Enter your name and choose Join meeting to enter the family room.
  3. Your camera and microphone start off. Turn on audio or video when you are ready to speak with the family.

3. Family Event Contributions (contributions.html)

  1. Open Family Contributions and review the ongoing event and its instructions.
  2. Check the verified treasurer’s photo, full name, and MTN number before paying.
  3. Enter your name, MTN number, and amount. Tap Open Dialer to start the MTN MoMo USSD flow, or dial the displayed *182*1*1*...# code yourself.
  4. Complete the payment in MTN MoMo, return to the page, then click Submit Payment so Umukomite can verify it.
  5. Join the event discussion by adding your name and comment in the comments section.

4. Contacting Umukomite & WhatsApp Group (contact.html)

  1. Open Contact Umukomite and enter your name and message to send a note directly to leadership.
  2. To join the official family group, tap the WhatsApp group button in the navigation when it appears.
